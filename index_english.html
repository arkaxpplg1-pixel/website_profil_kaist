Welcome to KAIST

KAIST is the first higher education institution in Korea to place a deep emphasis on science and engineering research. Established in 1971 with support from the United States, this campus has the primary mission of training experts who can drive the development of Korea's technology industry. KAIST's main campus is located in the Daejeon Daedeok Research and Development Special Zone, which is the largest research and development (R&D) hub in Korea. This campus environment, surrounded by hundreds of government and private research institutions, strongly supports academic activities. In addition, KAIST also has a Seoul campus focused on business and management. As an institution ranked in the global university Top 50, KAIST is famous for its competitive curriculum. Interestingly, most of the lectures here are conducted in English to facilitate the integration of international students.
